Pradayini Foundation Overview

Pradayini Foundation is a registered non-profit organisation committed to menstrual hygiene awareness, women empowerment, environmental care, and community wellbeing. We focus on communities where silence, stigma, and lack of menstrual health access still impact girls and women daily.

Our approach combines school-based awareness, women group sessions, and visible public campaigns led by volunteers and local partners. We design each intervention around lived ground situations so behavior change is practical, respectful, and sustained.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To break menstrual taboos through awareness, community partnerships, and practical health education that improves dignity and wellbeing.

Vision

To build an informed and equitable society where every girl and woman can access hygiene knowledge, confidence, and opportunities.

Read more: Mission aligned with recent government reports

Our mission planning is informed by recent government evidence, including school access trends (UDISE+ 2023-24), public health priorities (MoHFW Annual Report 2024-25), and environmental sustainability findings (India State of Forest Report 2023).

Our Theory of Change

Step 01

Build Awareness at Scale

Run sustained awareness in schools and communities where girls still face period stigma, fear, and poor access to health information.

Step 02

Strengthen Local Partnerships

Collaborate with institutions, volunteers, and women leaders to build trust, expand outreach, and normalize open conversation.

Step 03

Enable Community Action

Support women and youth to lead local initiatives, peer education circles, and awareness campaigns in their own communities.

Step 04

Sustain Long-Term Impact

Create repeatable, low-cost models that communities can continue with local champions and periodic technical support.
